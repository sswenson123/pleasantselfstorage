Frequently Asked Questions

How much does storage cost in Hackensack, MN?

Outdoor parking spaces (12×40 ft) are $40/month. Indoor enclosed units range from $65/month (8×11) up to $150/month (11×28). All rentals are month-to-month with no long-term contract.

Is there a contract or minimum rental period?

No contract — all rentals are month-to-month. There's a one-time $20 administrative fee at move-in, but you can leave any time without penalty.

Can I store a boat or pontoon indoors?

Yes. Indoor units up to 11×28 fit most fishing boats and pontoons up to about 24 ft on a trailer. The 10×24 and 11×26 units are the most popular for pontoons. For longer rigs, the 12×40 outdoor space handles oversized trailers.

What if the size I want is waitlisted?

Availability changes often — tenants move in and out regularly. Contact us to get on the notification list for a specific size. We'll reach out as soon as one opens up.

Do you serve Walker, Longville, or Pine River?

Yes — Pleasant Lake Storage is in Hackensack, about 15–20 minutes from Walker and Longville and convenient to Backus and Pine River. We're right off County Road 45 with easy access for trailers.
