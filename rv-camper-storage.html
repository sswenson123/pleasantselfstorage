Keep the Camper at the Lakes

Between Leech Lake, Ten Mile Lake, and the Chippewa National Forest, this is camper country — and your rig deserves better than street parking.

If your camper spends its summers between Hackensack, Walker, Backus, and Pine River, storing it up here beats towing it down Highway 371 after every weekend. Our gated lot on County Road 45 gives you a 12×40 space for $40/month — room for travel trailers, fifth wheels, Class A/B/C motorhomes, and toy haulers — with wide lanes for easy backing and hookup.

Many townships and HOAs restrict RV parking in driveways, and an uncovered rig at home is an invitation for weather damage and break-ins. Behind our fence, it's out of sight and inside a tenant-only gate — and with 24/7 access, you can hook up at sunrise Friday and drop it off Sunday night.
